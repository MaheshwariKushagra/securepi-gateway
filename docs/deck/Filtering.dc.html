FILTERING & PRIVACY
06 / 11

Two tiers of filtering, and no way around them.

Tier 1 blocks ad, tracker and threat domains for every device at the DNS layer. Tier 2 is opt-in and surgical — it removes first-party YouTube ads and decrypts nothing else.

TIER 1 DNS filtering — every device
656,735 rules · 5 curated lists
Refreshed daily, plus an offline threat-intel list. Every lookup is logged and attributed to a device.
Anti-bypass — devices can't route DNS around it:
DoT blocked known DoH blocked QUIC blocked canary domains
TIER 2 HTTPS inspection — opt-in
A selective mitmproxy addon strips first-party YouTube ads for enrolled devices only. It is off after every reboot and enabled deliberately, per device.
What it decrypts
YouTube ad traffic on enrolled devices
Everything else — passed through untouched
100% third-party ad blocking (10/10 fixed test domains)
