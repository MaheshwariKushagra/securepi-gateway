THE PROBLEM
01 / 11

A small office needs three things it usually buys separately — and can't afford.

Content filtering, an intrusion-detection system, and a security console are normally three products, three subscriptions, and staff to watch them. Most small networks simply go without.

Ads & trackers everywhere
Every device pulls ads and trackers on every network, and encrypted DNS lets them route around any simple block.
Intrusions go unseen
Port scans, brute force and malware beacons look like ordinary traffic without a sensor watching for the pattern.
Alerts nobody can read
A raw IDS emits thousands of alerts a day with no triage, no context, and no way to act on them.
