THE SOLUTION
02 / 11

Three roles, one machine.

SecurePi Gateway sits between the network and its uplink and does the work of a filter, an IDS, and a SOC — on a single laptop-class box with no Docker.

Filter
DNS filtering for every device from 656,735 rules across five curated lists. DoT, known DoH and QUIC are blocked so devices can't route around it. Selective HTTPS inspection removes first-party YouTube ads for devices that opt in — and decrypts nothing else.
Detect
The IDS, DNS resolver and inspection proxy write into one event store. A correlation engine runs 14 signal checks every 15 seconds, merges repeats, and links incidents into campaigns mapped to MITRE ATT&CK — turning 15,884 raw events into 10 incidents in a day.
Respond
A security-operations console: live dashboard, incident workbench with an evidence chain and playbooks, threat hunting, per-device policy, one-click quarantine, explainable risk scores, a weekly PDF report and a full audit trail.
