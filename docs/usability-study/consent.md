# Information and consent

**What this is.** A test of a home-network security control panel (SecurePi
Gateway), part of a student project. We want to find where it is confusing.

**What you'll do.** About 30 minutes: six short tasks on a laptop, thinking
aloud, then a ten-question form. The control panel shows an **invented**
network - nothing you do affects a real one.

**What we record.** Whether each task was completed, how long it took, and
notes on where you looked and what you said. No audio, video or screen
recording. You are identified only as a number (P1, P2, ...); we ask your
age band and how technical you'd say you are, nothing else.

**How it's used.** The results go into the project's evaluation report,
anonymously. The notes are kept with the project and not shared beyond it.

**Your choice.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without
giving a reason, and ask for your answers to be removed afterwards.
